What is a
crocodilian?

A crocodilian is any member of the reptile order Crocodilia. The name covers four familiar groups—not only crocodiles.

AlligatorsCrocodilesCaimansGharials

They are semi-aquatic archosaurs, making birds their closest living relatives. All share armored skin, powerful tails, four-chambered hearts, attentive parenting, and senses tuned to the waterline.

84 million yearsModern lineages reach deep into the Late Cretaceous.

26 living speciesRecognized here across three living families.

One shared designMany different jaws, habitats, diets, and survival stories.
